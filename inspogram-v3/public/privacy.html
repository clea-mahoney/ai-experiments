Inspogram

Privacy

Updated November 10, 2026

Inspogram is free. It has no ads, no tracking, and never sells your data.

What Inspogram stores

  • Your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.
  • What you save: photos, links, notes, titles, tags, and boards.
  • How you arrange cards on your boards.

Only you can see your saves. There is no sharing or public profile.

Who handles it

  • Google Firebase runs sign-in and stores your account and saves.
  • Google Fonts serves the typeface, so Google sees that your browser loaded it.
  • Title suggestions. When you write a note, its text is sent through a small relay on Cloudflare to a free AI model on OpenRouter, which suggests a title. Free model providers may keep what they receive and use it to improve their models. Photos and links are never sent. If you'd rather not send a note, don't write private details in it.

Briefs

"Make a brief" builds text on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it into an AI tool yourself.

Demo mode

The demo keeps everything in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

Deleting your data

Open the account menu and choose Delete account. That permanently removes your account and every save, board, and photo. To remove one save, open it and choose Delete.
